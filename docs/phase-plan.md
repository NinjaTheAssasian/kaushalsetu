# Implementation Phases

## Phase 1
Scaffold, database connection, frontend shell, environment setup.

## Phase 2
Authentication, RBAC, APAAR identity abstraction, learner/training-provider/employer/admin foundation.

## Phase 3A
Training operations: attendance, assessments, assessment results, learner progress, and test isolation.

## Phase 3B
Certification: eligibility, certificate issuance, certificate verification, certificate-to-skill mapping, and certification career events.

## Phase 3C
Longitudinal learner outcomes: 3/6/12-month check-ins and a combined career timeline.

## Phase 4
Employer portal, employment verification, job requirements, and employer skill feedback.

## Phase 5
Government outcome intelligence dashboard, retention, regional analytics, and skill supply vs. demand.

## Phase 6
Gemini skill-gap engine, curriculum-to-industry analysis, AI analytics assistant, maps, end-to-end demo data, security hardening, and polish.
